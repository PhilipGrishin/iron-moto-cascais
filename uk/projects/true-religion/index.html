Історія

True Religion — the project.

Some custom projects are remembered for technical complexity. Others are remembered for the feeling they create. True Religion belongs to the second category — a motorcycle built not only as a machine, but as a statement of style, freedom and personal identity. It reflects the side of motorcycle culture where the road, the rider and the character of the bike matter just as much as the build itself.

True Religion was shaped by a combination of strong cultural references: denim culture, classic Harley-Davidson spirit, and the rebellious atmosphere of the open road. That combination gives the motorcycle its identity. It feels deeply connected to the classic language of American custom motorcycles, while still carrying the unmistakable authorship of Iron Custom Motors.

One of the defining qualities of True Religion is its timeless custom silhouette. The project carries the energy of a classic chopper-inspired build, but it avoids becoming a copy of something familiar. Instead, it translates that heritage into a motorcycle with its own mood and presence — strong, direct and immediately recognizable. It works through atmosphere as much as through design.

True Religion remains relevant because it is built around ideas that do not age: freedom, road spirit, strong visual attitude and authentic custom identity. It reminds us that the most memorable motorcycles are not always the loudest or the most extreme — sometimes they are the ones that capture a way of life with complete honesty.
