# Tubeless conversion for spoked wheels: how we do it

[IMAGE: hero — sealed spoked rim / converted wheel at ICM | ALT: "Tubeless conversion of a spoked motorcycle wheel at Iron Custom Motors, Cascais"]

A classic spoked wheel is one of the best-looking things in motorcycling — and one of the most old-fashioned. The spoke nipples pierce the rim, so it can't hold air on its own: it needs an inner tube. A punctured tube deflates fast, and repairing it means pulling the wheel and the tube. Meanwhile, practically the whole modern motorcycle world rides tubeless: safer in a puncture, more convenient, far easier to repair. At Iron Custom Motors in Cascais (Lisbon) we seal spoked rims with polyurethane, so classic, retro and custom bikes keep the spoked look and gain tubeless behaviour. Here's how it's done.

## The video

Five and a half minutes: the full conversion, tyre-off to balanced-to-zero, on a Royal Enfield Bear 650. Watch it first, or read on and come back.

[VIDEO: Tubeless conversion of spoked wheels — 16:9, self-hosted]

## First, the spokes

Wheels apart, rim tape off, and the work starts with spoke tension. First by ear: every spoke should ring at roughly the same high pitch, none standing out — that matters for balance. Then a spoke wrench confirms no slack remains; wheel stability depends on it. Critical caveat: we only check the tension, never overtighten. Then a full wash and meticulous degrease — near-surgical cleanliness. The wheels on camera came off a Royal Enfield Bear 650, the same bike as our [scrambler build](/blog/royal-enfield-bear-650-scrambler-build/).

## Three thin layers and 36 hours

The sealant rule is hard: never construction or sanitary silicone — it shrinks over time, dries out, cracks. We use single-component polyurethane made for load-bearing parts under constant vibration: resistant to aggressive environments and UV, elastic for decades. It cures on atmospheric moisture, so it goes on in three thin stages of 1–2 mm: the spoke cavities first, then two top layers, with 12–16 hours of polymerisation between them and space left for the valve stem. On camera, the first layer had dried after 16 hours; at 36 hours both were dry, smooth and neat. Not fast. Worth it.

## The result

We also fit angled air valves — inflating past a disc or hub is far easier at an angle. Then assembly and the leak test: every spoke, and the valve too. Final touch: balanced to zero, and the wheels go back on. The coating stays elastic for decades, shrugs off vibration, doesn't crack. And the method scales: the video shows a classic thirty-inch spoked wheel converted to tubeless — the same machines behind our [motorcycle tyre service](/motorcycle-tyre-service/) handle wheels up to 30 inches.

## FAQ

**Can spoked motorcycle wheels be converted to tubeless?**
Yes. The rim is sealed from the inside with polyurethane, spokes included, and holds air without a tube. The classic look stays — only the angled valve gives it away.

**How long does a tubeless conversion take?**
Three thin layers with 12–16 hours of curing between them — about 36 hours in total, plus leak test and balancing. Days, not hours.

**Would silicone work instead of polyurethane?**
No — silicone sealants shrink, dry out and crack. We use single-component polyurethane made for load-bearing, vibration-exposed parts.

**How long does the seal last?**
Decades. The polyurethane stays elastic, shrugs off vibration and doesn't crack; every wheel is leak-tested spoke by spoke.

# Rodas de raios sem câmara de ar: como fazemos a conversão tubeless

## Primeiro, os raios

Rodas desmontadas, fita protetora fora, e o trabalho começa pela tensão dos raios. Primeiro de ouvido: cada raio deve soar aproximadamente na mesma nota aguda, sem nenhum destoar — isso importa para o equilíbrio. Depois a chave de raios confirma que não há folga nenhuma; disso depende a estabilidade da roda. Ressalva crítica: só verificamos a tensão, nunca apertamos em excesso. Segue-se lavagem completa e desengorduramento minucioso — limpeza quase cirúrgica. As rodas em cena saíram de uma Royal Enfield Bear 650, a mesma mota da nossa [preparação scrambler](/pt/blog/royal-enfield-bear-650-scrambler-build/).

## Três camadas finas e 36 horas

No vedante a regra é rígida: nunca silicones de construção ou sanitários — encolhem com o tempo, secam e estalam. Usamos poliuretano monocomponente concebido para peças estruturais sob vibração constante: resistente a ambientes agressivos e aos UV, elástico durante décadas. Cura com a humidade atmosférica, por isso aplica-se em três fases finas de 1–2 mm: primeiro as cavidades dos raios, depois duas camadas de cobertura, com 12–16 horas de polimerização entre elas e espaço livre para a válvula. No vídeo, a primeira camada secou ao fim de 16 horas; às 36 estavam ambas secas, lisas e limpas. Não é rápido. Vale a pena.

## O resultado

Instalamos ainda válvulas anguladas: encher um pneu com o disco ou o cubo no caminho é muito mais fácil em ângulo. Segue-se a montagem e o teste de fugas — raio a raio, e a válvula também. Toque final: equilibragem a zero, e as rodas voltam à mota. O revestimento mantém-se elástico durante décadas, não teme a vibração, não estala. E o método aguenta escala: o vídeo mostra uma roda clássica de trinta polegadas convertida — as mesmas máquinas do nosso serviço de [montagem de pneus de mota](/pt/montagem-de-pneus-mota/) trabalham com rodas até 30 polegadas.

## FAQ

**Чи можна перевести спицьовані колеса на безкамерні шини?**
Так. Обід герметизується поліуретаном зсередини, разом зі спицями, і тримає повітря без камери. Зовні колесо лишається класичним — конверсію видає хіба що кутовий вентиль.

**Скільки триває конверсія і чому так довго?**
Герметик полімеризується від атмосферної вологи: три тонкі шари з паузами 12–16 годин, загалом близько 36 годин, плюс перевірка й балансування. Рахуйте днями.

**Чи підійде силікон замість поліуретану?**
Ні. Силіконові герметики всідаються, висихають і тріскаються. Ми працюємо однокомпонентним поліуретаном для навантажених деталей під вібрацією.

**Наскільки це довговічно?**
Десятиліттями: шар лишається еластичним, не боїться вібрації, не тріскається. Кожне колесо перевіряється по кожній спиці.

**А великі колеса — наприклад, на 30 дюймів?**
Так. У відео — класичне колесо на 30 дюймів, переведене тим самим методом. Обладнання працює з колесами до 30 дюймів.
