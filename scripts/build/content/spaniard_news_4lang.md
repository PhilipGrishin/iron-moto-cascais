# A new project on the site: Spaniard

We have published the page of our latest finished build — Spaniard, a 2002 Harley-Davidson V-Rod that came into the Cascais workshop as a bike with history and left as one matte black object, rebuilt from the frame out and serviced from the valves out over six months, May to October 2026.

[IMAGE: hero — Spaniard, matte black V-Rod 2002 rebuilt by Iron Custom Motors]
ALT: Spaniard — matte black Harley-Davidson V-Rod 2002 with a 240 rear tyre, the new project of Iron Custom Motors in Cascais

## What the project is

A first-year V-Rod on the running gear of a 2012 model — fork, swingarm, wheels, offset hub, 240 at the back and 120/70 R19 at the front. We took it apart completely, painted every paintable part, adapted and fitted the lower body kit with new mesh, re-upholstered the seat in Alcantara and carbon-pattern leather, and put it back together with Kellermann lights, Galfer wave discs, braided lines, new Metzeler Cruisetec tyres, taller risers and bar-end mirrors.

Underneath the paint it received the full service a twenty-year-old Revolution engine deserves: valve clearances, a new clutch, rebuild kits in the calipers and master cylinders, wheel and steering-head bearings, fork service, oil, filters and coolant. The whole story, section by section, is on the project page: [Spaniard](/projects/spaniard/).

## Why we show the service, not only the paint

Most custom pages show the surface. We decided to list the mechanical work in the same detail as the bodywork, because that is what a rebuild at Iron Custom Motors means: the bike has to ride as well as it looks, and the owner has to know exactly what was done. That is the same principle as our written estimate before any job — custom or [regular service](/motorcycle-service/).

## O que é o projeto

Uma V-Rod do primeiro ano sobre a parte ciclística de um modelo de 2012 — forqueta, braço oscilante, rodas, cubo com deslocamento, 240 atrás e 120/70 R19 à frente. Por baixo da pintura recebeu a revisão completa que um motor Revolution com vinte anos merece: folgas de válvulas, embraiagem nova, kits de reparação nas pinças e nas bombas, rolamentos das rodas e da coluna de direção, revisão da forqueta, óleo, filtros e líquido de refrigeração.
