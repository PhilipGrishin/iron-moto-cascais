# A Saturday BBQ with friends of the workshop

On Saturday, 22 August 2026, we fired up the grill at Iron Custom Motors in Cascais (Lisbon). No launch, no sales pitch, just a BBQ for friends of the workshop and the clients who have become part of its circle. Everyone was invited personally.

[IMAGE: hero — BBQ party at the ICM workshop]
ALT: BBQ party at the Iron Custom Motors workshop in Cascais, with guests and motorcycles

## How the day went

Simple, the way a good workshop day should be. Barbecue on the grill, cold drinks and beer, snacks on the tables, music in the background. And motorcycles everywhere — the yard was full of them, which is exactly how we like it.

It turned into a proper family afternoon too. Wives came along, kids ran around between the bikes, and the whole thing stayed relaxed from the first hour to the last. Nobody was in a hurry to leave.

## What we talked about

Motorcycles, mostly. That tends to happen here. The team talked with guests about the projects currently in the works and hinted at what's coming next, without giving too much away. The rest was the kind of conversation that only happens when riders stand around a grill: routes, machines, plans for the season.

That is really what the lounge and the permanent exhibition of our championship customs are for. The workshop was never meant to be a place you only visit when something needs fixing. Days like this prove the point.

This wasn't a single event to tick a box. We plan to get friends of the workshop together like this every few months. No fixed calendar, no ticket page. If you're part of the ICM circle, or want to be, you'll hear about the next one. Follow us and watch this space.

## The door is open in between

You don't have to wait for the next BBQ.
