The story

Inspirium — the project.

Inspirium is the bike that put Iron Custom Motors on the international map. In August 2017, our team brought it to the Bonneville Salt Flats in Utah — the legendary salt-flats course where the world's fastest two-wheel records have been set since 1914.

The class: 350APS-VG — partial streamliner, 350cc, push-rod, gasoline, vintage. A class with very specific rules, with very specific bikes from a very specific era — and the rules don't bend for anyone.

The bike held the line. Best run: 99.764 mph. New record in the class.

It is one thing to repair motorcycles. It is another thing to build one that can go faster than any motorcycle in its class has ever gone — and then take it across an ocean to prove it.
