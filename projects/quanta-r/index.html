The story

Quanta R — the project.

Quanta R was conceived as a vehicle with no direct analogue in the market. Not a conventional custom car, not a motorcycle-derived machine in the usual sense, but an entirely new concept. The idea: combine the aggression, adrenaline and open emotional character of a motorcycle with the stability, control and confidence of a high-performance car.

The project was created for presentation at the Geneva International Motor Show and the Goodwood Festival of Speed. Public descriptions point to a Subaru-based all-wheel-drive platform, a tuned 2.5-liter turbocharged boxer engine, and power adjustable up to 600 horsepower. Combined with low vehicle weight, the project delivers hypercar-level acceleration and a completely unique driving experience.

The body was developed as a hand-built aluminum structure with a futuristic silhouette and dramatic road presence. Open-wheel proportions, aggressive stance, sculptural surfacing, carbon elements, forged wheels, oversized tires — Quanta R was never intended to be just a showpiece. It was designed as a fully realized vision of what happens when engineering ambition is not limited by standard formats.

Years after its presentation, Quanta R remains an important reference point for those who follow custom engineering and boundary-pushing builds. The project reflects exactly what makes Iron Custom Motors different: deep technical competence, artistic discipline and the courage to build something the world has not seen before.
