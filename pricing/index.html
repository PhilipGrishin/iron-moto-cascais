07 · Customizing & community

Customizing and community

Bespoke motorcycle builds and a space where a community forms around the workshop.

From concept to championship build

Full bespoke motorcycle builds — from concept and technical brief to final assembly and entry into international championships. The same engineering approach that earned ICM the AMD World Champions title, the world speed record at Bonneville and the win at the BMW Motorrad Customizing Championship. About these prices

All prices are in euro and include applicable taxes and fees. Prices are indicative: the final amount is determined after diagnostics and depends on the make, model, technical condition of the motorcycle and the actual scope of work. The scheduled-service price includes all necessary consumables, except the air-filter replacement on Indian, BMW, Moto Guzzi, Ducati, Japan and KTM models. For other work, the cost of parts and consumables is not included unless stated otherwise. This price list is for information only and does not constitute a binding offer.
