Where we come from

A brand built by real builds.

Iron Custom Motors didn't start with marketing. It started with a stubborn passion for motorcycles and bold projects. Over the years the brand grew from a local workshop to a name that carries world-champion machines, engineering solutions and projects discussed beyond a single country.

That's why custom and special projects are not a decorative add-on for us — they are a natural extension of the brand's philosophy. We think deeper than catalog modifications and turn the rider's idea into a coherent, technically and visually strong result.
