Why Ducati owners come to us

Desmo done properly.

Iron Custom Motors is an independent Ducati specialist in Cascais. Desmodromic valve train, complex electronics packages, semi-active suspension, dry and wet clutches — we know what makes each Ducati family tick. The bike that scares most independent workshops is the bike we work on every week.

Desmo service is where most Ducati owners get hit with a bill they didn't expect at the dealer. We do desmo service correctly, on schedule, at independent-workshop rates — and we document the work in writing so you know exactly what was done.

We service the modern Ducati lineup: Monster (air-cooled and water-cooled), Panigale V2/V4, Multistrada V2/V4/V4 Rally, Scrambler family, Streetfighter V2/V4, Diavel, Hypermotard, SuperSport. From daily street to full track-day prep for Estoril.

Typical Ducati issues we know

Where the desmo bites.

Years of Ducati service mean we know the platform's failure modes. Caught early, they're routine fixes. Caught late, they're expensive.

Valve clearance drift on desmo

Often missed because owners delay service. We measure properly and adjust with the right shim selection — not just confirm the symptom.

Ride-by-wire throttle desync

Common on modern Ducati. We re-sync with DDS and verify the operation cleanly via live data.

Battery / charging on bikes parked over winter

Modern Ducati can self-drain with all electronics asleep. We diagnose and recommend a proper trickle charger setup.

Dry clutch basket wear (older Monsters)

Signature dry-clutch noise that hides real wear. We measure the basket clearances properly.

Electronic suspension calibration errors

Sachs / Marzocchi self-calibration faults. We reset with the right tool and verify damping operation.

FAQ

Common questions.

How often is desmo service really needed?
Depends on the model and year. Older Ducati: 24,000 km. Modern Panigale V4 / Multistrada V4: 30,000 km on intake valves, 60,000 km on exhaust valves. We confirm against your bike's specific schedule before quoting.
How much does a desmo service cost at Iron Custom Motors?
Per-model price — from €750 for older Monsters, up to €1,500+ for Panigale V4 with full inspection. Compared to a dealer bill, typically 30–40% less. Sent in writing before work starts.
Can you flash a Termignoni / Akrapovič map onto my Ducati?
Yes — we install the exhaust (full system or slip-on) and upload the matched ECU map. Termignoni race kit, Akrapovič with eCU, third-party maps as required. Dyno-verified result documented.
Do you do track-day prep for Panigale / Streetfighter / Multistrada?
Yes — suspension setup, brake lines and pads, slick tire fitment, ride height, ECU map tuned to circuit profile. We've prepared bikes for Estoril, Portimão and Jarama.
Are you an authorized Ducati dealer?
No — independent Ducati workshop. The advantage is transparent pricing (desmo at independent rates, not dealer rates) and freedom to use OEM Ducati Performance or top-tier aftermarket. We work with all major Ducati distributors and have full catalog access.
